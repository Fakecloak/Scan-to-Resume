# Scan to Resume

### Tired of sitting at your PC? Scan it and continue on your phone.

A simple browser extension that lets you **resume what you're doing on your PC from your phone** using a QR code.

🎥 **Watching YouTube?**
Scan the QR code and continue the video from the **same timestamp** on your phone.

🌐 **On a website?**
Scan it and open the same page on your phone.

### How it works

**PC → Scan QR → Phone → Continue**

No copying links. No searching again. Just scan and go.

## Installation

1. Download the latest release.
2. Extract the ZIP.
3. Open `chrome://extensions`
4. Enable **Developer mode**.
5. Click **Load unpacked**.
6. Select the extracted folder.

## Best with YouTube

Made for those moments when you're halfway through a video and just don't want to sit at your PC anymore.

**Scan it. Pick up your phone. Keep watching.**
